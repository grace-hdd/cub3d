# Task 2.2 – Check the map is closed

File: `src/parsing/check_map.c`. Called from `main` after `parse_player`, before `init_player_view`.

## `check_map_closed(game)`
Visits every cell of the rectangular grid (1.6 padded short rows with spaces). For each walkable cell (`0` or the player `N/S/E/W`, see `is_walkable`) `cell_closed` requires all 4 neighbors (left, right, up, down) to be:
- **inside the grid** (`neighbor_ok` rejects x/y outside `0..width-1` / `0..height-1`), so a `0` or the player on the border is open;
- **not a space**, so a `0` touching a space, or touching the padding of a short row, is open.

Any failure → `the map is not closed by walls`. Walls (`1`) and spaces are never checked, so spaces around the map and spaces inside a wall block are accepted.

Only 4-neighbors are checked (as in the plan); diagonals do not matter because a diagonal gap cannot be walked through by the player.
