# Task 3.2 – Floor and ceiling

File: `src/render/background.c`.

## `draw_background(game)`
Fills the whole image buffer with two colors: rows `0 .. WIN_H/2 - 1` get `cfg.ceiling`, rows `WIN_H/2 .. WIN_H - 1` get `cfg.floor`. The colors are the packed `0xRRGGBB` ints produced by the parser (task 1.4), written with `put_pixel` (3.1), so no conversion is needed. With `WIN_H` = 720 the ceiling is rows 0-359 and the floor rows 360-719.

It is called from `run_game` (3.3) once, right before `mlx_put_image_to_window`. When the raycaster exists (Phase 4) it will be the first step of each frame, and the wall columns are drawn on top of it, so the rest of the screen keeps the floor/ceiling colors.

## Tests
`tests/test_background.c` (needs a display, ASan + UBSan, no errors or leaks): top-left and top-right pixels have the ceiling color, the last ceiling row and the first floor row (at `WIN_H / 2`) are right, and the bottom-right pixel has the floor color. `tests/test_exit.c` still passes with the background drawn.
`cc -g -std=gnu17 -fsanitize=address,undefined -Iinc -Ilibft -Iminilibx-linux tests/test_background.c src/render/window.c src/render/background.c src/utils/error.c libft/libft.a minilibx-linux/libmlx.a -lXext -lX11 -lm`
The pixel buffer is what is tested; check the real window by eye once with `./cub3D tests/maps/valid/basic.cub` (red-orange ceiling above a darker orange floor).
